# Nano Enhanced Eutectic Phase Change Materials for Vaccine Cold Chain

**Caprylic acid and lauryl alcohol with boron nitride nanoparticles for storage between 2 and 8 °C.**

Winter internship | NIT Tiruchirappalli | November to December 2025

[Back to all projects](../README.md)

## Overview

Most vaccines must stay between 2 and 8 °C from the manufacturer to the patient, and a break in this cold chain can spoil an entire batch. Phase change materials help because they absorb and release latent heat at a nearly constant temperature, so a container holds its temperature for longer when power or refrigeration is interrupted.

Organic fatty acids are attractive for this range, but they conduct heat poorly, which slows charging and discharging. This project developed a eutectic mixture of caprylic acid and lauryl alcohol for the 2 to 8 °C range, and added boron nitride nanoparticles to improve its thermal conductivity.

## Work

- Developed a nano enhanced eutectic phase change material from caprylic acid, lauryl alcohol and boron nitride nanoparticles
- Characterized its thermal behavior for vaccine cold chain logistics

## Application

Passive cooling for vaccine carriers and storage boxes, particularly where reliable electricity is not available.
